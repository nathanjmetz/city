Play Neighbors on your computer

Free for Mac and Windows. No installer and no admin password. It looks sharper than the browser version, sets its own graphics to fit your computer, and updates itself.

Download for MacApple silicon and Intel, about 70 MB Download for WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit, about 120 MB

Same towns and same neighbors as the browser. Your invite code works in both.

First time on a Mac

We don't pay Apple to check the app, so the first time you open it the Mac stops it with this box:

  1. Click Done. (Not Move to Trash.)
  2. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy & Security in the sidebar.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Under “Allow applications from” a new line says Neighbors was blocked. Click Open Anyway there.
    Security
    Allow applications fromApp Store & Known Developers
    “Neighbors” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  4. A box asks again. Click Open Anyway and type your Mac password (or use Touch ID).

You only do this once. After that it opens like any app and updates itself. The Open Anyway line shows up only for about an hour after the Mac blocks the app, so if it's missing, double-click Neighbors again first.

Or one line in Terminal, on your own Mac

This downloads the app into your Applications folder and opens it, with no warning boxes. Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space and type Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fL https://github.com/nathanjmetz/city/releases/download/desktop/Neighbors-mac.zip -o /tmp/Neighbors.zip && ditto -xk /tmp/Neighbors.zip ~/Applications && open ~/Applications/Neighbors.app

Next time, open Neighbors from Launchpad or Spotlight. Only do this on a Mac that's yours. The warning box only shows for files a web browser downloaded, and this is the same file.

First time on Windows

  1. Double-click Neighbors-windows.exe. Nothing gets installed.
  2. If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

You only do this once.

A work or school computer?

Some are set up to block apps from outside the company. If yours is, please follow its rules and ask your IT team, or just play in the browser. It needs nothing installed.
