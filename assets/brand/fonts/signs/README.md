# Sign lettering

Fonts for words painted or mounted on signs in the world (Families, SignText).
Each matches a real small-town sign style:

| Style | Font | Real signs it stands for | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| slab | Alfa Slab One | painted slab serif on feed stores, lumber yards, farm signs | OFL 1.1 |
| serif | Bitter Bold | banks, insurance agents, funeral homes, law offices | OFL 1.1 |
| condensed | Oswald Bold | garages, taverns, machine shops, oil and stone companies | OFL 1.1 |
| script | Yellowtail | 1950s brush script on diners, bakeries, salons, florists | Apache 2.0 (Astigmatic) |
| franklin | Libre Franklin Bold | 1960s grocers, drug stores, realtors | OFL 1.1 |
| gothic | Overpass (../Overpass.ttf) | the plain modern sans a business moves to when it redoes its look | OFL 1.1 |

From the fontsource packages (Google Fonts builds), Latin subset.
