# Real-life textures (desktop app only)

From Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): free for any use,
no credit required, credited here anyway. Fetched by `tools/textures/fetch.py` from
`tools/textures/list.json`. The Web export leaves `art/pbr/` out.

- `brown_mud` (2k, 1.3 x 1.3 m): muddy creek and pond banks (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud by Rob Tuytel
- `farm_soil` (2k, 2.0 x 2.0 m): worked field soil between the crop rows (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/farm_soil by Amal Kumar
- `gravel_floor_02` (2k, 2.0 x 2.0 m): crushed limestone county roads painted on the land (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_02 by Jenelle van Heerden, Dimitrios Savva
- `leafy_grass` (2k, 2.0 x 2.0 m): pasture, lawn and ditch grass (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass by Charlotte Baglioni
- `leaves_forest_ground` (2k, 1.3 x 1.3 m): woods floor leaf litter (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/leaves_forest_ground by Dario Barresi, Dimitrios Savva
- `snow_02` (2k, 2.0 x 2.0 m): snow on open ground (TerrainView). https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02 by Rob Tuytel
